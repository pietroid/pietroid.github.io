# Enduroids (2009)

I started coding at the age of 10 Years. I started simply with a computer, a Macromedia Flash Program, my father teaching me somethings and a creative child mind. 

After all those years in my career I think what I always liked is to build nice interfaces, animations and cool user experiences. Software Engineering is just a justification for it.

## The Game

For those who remember Flash (R.I.P.), it was a very promising tool built for Animators. But it increasingly got more features, including its own language: `ActionScript`. There were I learned key principles of OOP and how to actually build something cool out of it.

With that I built multiple experiments, playful animations and mini games. I had the luck that one of my games is preserved until today, and is playable. It's a mix of Enduro and Asteroids.

You can check it out [here](https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/pietroid/enduroids), hope you enjoy it.
